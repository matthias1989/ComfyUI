KEY FINDING:

The 59 pure loops are tiny micro-loops (< 2cm) around small features
(hair strands, fingers, eye sockets) — NOT neck/arm/leg border rings.

A real neck ring on a human-scale model would be ~30cm diameter.

=== WHAT THIS MEANS ===
Rodin's UV seaming is the result of an automated UV unwrap of a complex
character (hair, clothing detail, skin texture regions). The seams are
placed wherever the UV needs to split — not following anatomical seam
lines a rigger/modeler would draw manually.

=== SOLUTION ===
Generate ANATOMICAL seams on Trellis directly:
  1. Segment mesh into body parts (head/torso/L-arm/R-arm/L-leg/R-leg)
  2. Mark seam edges at body-part boundaries -> gives clean rings
  3. Run UV unwrap -> produces proper UV islands per body part
This approach is topology-independent and works on any isotropic remesh.
